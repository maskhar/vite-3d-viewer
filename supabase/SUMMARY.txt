📊 DATABASE STRUCTURE

models_catalog table:
  • id (UUID) - Auto generated
  • name, category, description
  • model_filename (reference to Storage)
  • preview settings (camera, rotation, scale)
  • viewer settings (auto rotate, camera)
  • is_active (show/hide)
  • display_order (urutan tampilan)
  • timestamps (created_at, updated_at)
